About Me

I am a competitive chess player with over 10 years of experience and one of the strongest players in my age group nationally. As a passionate chess coach, I help players of all levels improve their game and develop a deeper appreciation for chess.

My journey in chess began at a young age, and through dedication and continuous learning, I achieved the prestigious FIDE Master title. This experience has given me unique insights into the learning process and the challenges players face at different stages of their development.

My students range from beginners to advanced players, and I take pride in helping them achieve their chess goals. Whether you're looking to understand basic principles or master complex strategies, I provide personalized guidance tailored to your needs.
